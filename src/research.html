What this establishes

The Lean library proves conditional finite-support statements: under explicit positivity, balance and symmetry assumptions, the constructed kernel has the stated stationarity or reversibility properties. Floating-point scaling is not a Lean proof of those premises. General continuous bridge existence, scaling-factor existence, KL optimality and solver correctness are outside the formal claim.
